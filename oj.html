A maritime salvage inspector boards a modern cargo freighter found drifting silently off the coast. The engines are idling, cargo holds are sealed, and coffee is still warm on the bridge, but the entire 22-person crew has disappeared without a single distress call.

Book Overview

The Ghost Wake is a tense maritime sci-fi thriller set in the freezing expanse of the North Atlantic. When maritime safety inspector Elena Reyes boards the drifting freighter Orion Trader, she expects an investigation into an abandoned vessel running on autopilot. Instead, she uncovers a deadly corporate experiment gone wrong: a mysterious, pressure-dependent abyssal organism dredged from the Molloy Deep that is rapidly consuming the ship's infrastructure, neural systems, and crew. Trapped between a relentless biological entity, a covert corporate salvage team willing to silence witnesses, and a looming oceanic storm, Elena must fight to survive and expose the truth before the ship is dragged into the abyss.
